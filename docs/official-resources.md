### What you'll need to apply

A complete application typically includes:

- A bachelor's degree (or equivalent) from an accredited institution
- Transcripts of your undergraduate and/or graduate education
- An official GMAT or GRE score
- An official TOEFL or IELTS score (for [international applicants](https://grad.umn.edu/admissions/application-instructions/international-student-resources/english-language-proficiency))
- A personal statement
- Three letters of recommendation
- A résumé/CV
- The application fee

This is a general summary, not a checklist to apply against — score minimums, GPA guidance, and any other current specifics are set and maintained on the official [Admission Requirements & Deadlines](https://carlsonschool.umn.edu/graduate/phd/admissions/requirements-deadlines) page.

### Key dates

- **Application deadline:** December 15 each year, for admission the following fall.
- **Admission decisions:** made on a rolling basis starting in late January, with most decisions made in February–March, and all decisions finalized by April 15 (the national graduate-admissions response deadline).
- **Standardized test scores:** must be no more than five years old as of your application date.

Exact dates (including any GMAT/GRE waiver-request deadline, which falls a few days before the application deadline) are set annually — always confirm against the official [Admission Requirements & Deadlines](https://carlsonschool.umn.edu/graduate/phd/admissions/requirements-deadlines) and [PhD FAQ](https://carlsonschool.umn.edu/graduate/phd/faq) pages.

### Program length

The program is designed to be completed in about five years — matching the five years of guaranteed funding below — though the official FAQ notes that actual time to completion typically ranges four to six years. Coursework is concentrated in the first two years; the written preliminary exam typically follows in the summer after year two, and the oral preliminary exam typically in year three, ahead of dissertation research.

## Funding

All admitted students are guaranteed **five years of full funding** (tuition plus stipend, through a combination of teaching/research assistantships and fellowships), as long as they remain in good academic standing. For current stipend amounts and details, see the official page:

- [PhD Tuition & Aid](https://carlsonschool.umn.edu/graduate/phd/tuition-aid) — current funding, stipend, and tuition information.
