# Information and Decision Sciences PhD Program

Welcome. This site is a **supplemental resource** for prospective students exploring the PhD program in **Information and Decision Sciences** at the [Carlson School of Management](https://carlsonschool.umn.edu/), [University of Minnesota](https://twin-cities.umn.edu/).

The Information and Decision Sciences PhD trains researchers who study how individuals, organizations, and technology intersect — drawing on computer science, economics, organizational behavior, and behavioral science. The University of Minnesota established one of the first doctoral programs in management information systems in the United States, and the Information and Decision Sciences department remains among the top-ranked programs worldwide.

## About the Information and Decision Sciences department

Information and Decision Sciences faculty conduct research spanning artificial intelligence and machine learning, social networks and crowdsourcing, IT strategy, and human–computer/robot interaction, among other areas. The department is home to *MIS Quarterly*, a leading journal in the field, and hosts a weekly research seminar featuring visiting scholars. Graduates go on to research and teaching careers at universities worldwide.

For the authoritative, up-to-date description of the program, visit the official [PhD in Information & Decision Sciences](https://carlsonschool.umn.edu/graduate/phd/concentrations/information-decision-sciences) page.
