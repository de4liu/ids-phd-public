# Changelog

All notable changes to this site are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-07-28

Initial public release.

### Added

- Home page introducing the site, with a prominent "this is not an official Carlson School or University of Minnesota website" disclaimer.
- "Considering the IDS PhD?" resource: what a research PhD is (and isn't), program fit (research orientation, career goals, preparation, full-time commitment), nontraditional academic/professional backgrounds, how to assess research fit, why the program can't pre-assess admission likelihood from an informal inquiry, and how to reach out — adapted from internal admissions-correspondence guidance and the CSOM PhD Handbook, filtered for a public audience.
- Official Resources page: verified links to the Carlson School and University of Minnesota pages for admissions requirements, faculty, funding, and international-applicant requirements, plus a summary of the application-materials checklist, key admissions dates, program length, and the five-year funding guarantee, sourced from and cited to the official pages.
- Understated Carlson maroon/gold visual theme, using only official brand colors (no Carlson/UMN logos or wordmarks, per the brand site's official-business-only usage restriction).
- mkdocs-material site scaffold, with local setup/preview/build/deploy instructions in the README.

### Changed

- Body links underlined by default, not just on hover, so they're distinguishable from regular text independent of color perception.
- Admissions contact split by purpose: the IDS PhD Coordinator for program-fit questions, the Carlson PhD Program Office for general application questions.
- Admissions-decision description aligned with official process language: department-level decisions overseen by the coordinator together with several faculty, not a single advisor.
